From the code it seems that the flag is being scrambled through a series of swaps. By keeping track the pair of indices that are being swapped around, the process can be reversed by reversing the array containing the swapped indices and running through the swaps on the reversed array, obtaining the flag for this challenge.


Solve script (in Python):
